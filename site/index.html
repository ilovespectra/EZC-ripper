EZC Ripper is a standalone desktop app for choosing one video or a whole playlist, selecting a folder, and managing downloads in a clear local queue.

EZC Ripper · local deckready

A tidy queue, not another cloud service.

Choose the output folder and format, then keep an eye on every active job from the app.

  • Queue single videos or playlists and follow separate progress and logs.
  • Cancel one job or the whole queue; playlist progress can resume when you add that playlist again.
  • Use the native folder picker and save MP4 video or MP3 audio directly on your Mac.
  • Optionally use EZC's saved YouTube session or explicitly choose a browser-cookie source.

This product

EZC Ripper

A local Mac YouTube downloader for MP4 video and MP3 audio. Files go to the folder you choose.

Different product

EZC Cloud Transfer

Transfers selected files from Google Drive and Google Photos to local storage. It is not the YouTube downloader.

Older project

Python EZC

The older Python/browser-extension project is separate. Its files are not installers for this native Mac app.

Local by design: downloads and optional session data stay on this Mac; EZC Ripper does not upload your files to a cloud-transfer service. It does not bypass DRM or access restrictions, and a playable video is not guaranteed to be downloadable.
